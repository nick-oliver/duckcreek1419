Duck Creek Masonic Lodge October 2025 Stated Meeting, Monday, October 13, 2025, 7:00pm.

At the September Stated meeting we reported on the 2024 Audit and approved the 2025-2026 Budget. We will also be assembling the Fantastic Teeth kits and getting them delivered. We are doing 6 elementary schools this year, Heather Glen, Classical Center at Vial, Williams, Herfurth, Toler and Shorehaven Elementary Schools. We also started the discussion on the upcoming Holiday Dinner in December and Christmas on the Square.

Blood Drives

We collected 27 units of blood at the Saturday, August 9 Blood Drive at the Mesquite Rodeo Convention Center, in conjunction with the Premier Gun Shows, benefitting the Texas Scottish Rite Children's Hospital.

Duck Creek Masonic Lodge is located at 600 N. 5th Street in Garland, TX
Stated Meetings are the second Monday of each Month at 7:00pm

The Duck Creek Education Foundation is a 501c(3) and is accepting donations for school supplies and scholarship awards. Scholarships will be awarded in May of this year, and we continue to collect for the School Supply Drive to support the financially distressed elementary school children within Garland Independent School District.
