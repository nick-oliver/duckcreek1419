Duck Creek Education Foundation

One Scholarship, totaling $1500, is given annually to a graduating senior who is of direct descent of a Duck Creek member (child, grandchild) from any highschool, irrespective of location. This scholarship is awarded in April or May, with funds distributed in two parts, one in the Fall and one in the Spring.

Two Scholarships, of $1000 each, are given annually to two graduating seniors from any of the seven GISD high schools, ie. Garland High School, North Garland High School, South Garland High School, Naaman Forest High School, Lakeview Centennial High School, Rowlett High School and Sachse High School. These scholarships are awarded in April or May, with funds distributed in two parts, one in the Fall and one in the Spring.

Scholarships

Our 2026-2027 Scholarships will be awarded at the May Stated Meeting of Duck Creek Masonic Lodge.

Duck Creek Masonic Lodge is located at 600 N. 5th Street in Garland, TX

Stated Meetings are the second Monday of each Month at 7:00pm

The Duck Creek Education Foundation is a 501c(3) and is accepting donations for school supplies and scholarship awards. Scholarships were awarded in May of 2025, and we continue to collect for the School Supply Drive to support the financially distressed elementary school children within Garland Independent School District.
